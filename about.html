Our Story

The view from outside the frame.

Most creatives are deep inside their work — the performer, the brand, the product. For the past eight years, the role here has been different: the lighting designer setting the stage for someone else's show. The photographer making a business look its best before stepping back. The AVL tech making sure every connection holds on the night it matters most. That outside perspective — watching how creative work actually lands — shapes everything about how these sites get built.

Photography taught us that brand perception is everything. Live production taught us that one overlooked detail can bring the whole system down. Real estate photography taught us to find the angle that sells. Commercial marketing taught us that how something feels to an audience matters just as much as how it looks. Every discipline added another layer to the same question: why does this work, and why does that not?

More than any of it — husband, dad, drummer, follower of Jesus — the work here starts with people. Not projects, not contracts. Just a genuine interest in helping small businesses look like what they actually are: good at what they do.
