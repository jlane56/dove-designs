About

Hi, I'm Justin.

Photographer. Lighting designer. Web builder. Peoria, IL.

My Story

Every detail matters.

I've spent the last eight years behind a camera. Commercial work, real estate, events — if it needed to be captured well, I showed up. That same eye for detail found its way into web design when I started building sites for small businesses here in Peoria, and it turned out the work wasn't all that different.

My background is in live production — I design lighting for shows and install full AVL systems in churches and concert venues across Central Illinois. In that world, a single overlooked connection can bring the whole system down on the night it matters most. I build websites the same way: every detail deliberate, every piece accounted for, nothing left to chance.

Mostly though, I just love people. I don't need a big reason to help. If I can make someone's day a little better — a business owner finally proud of their website, a church with a stage that feels professional — that's enough for me.
