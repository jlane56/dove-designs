Our Story

Always in the room.
Never the headliner.

For the past eight years, the job has been to make other people look good. Lighting designer. Photographer. AVL tech. Always behind the scenes, always on the outside of the creative work. Turns out that's a great place to learn what actually works. And what doesn't.

You spend enough time in that spot and you start to notice things. What makes a brand feel trustworthy. What makes a stage look expensive. What makes someone stop scrolling. Web design wasn't a big leap from there. Just a different screen to apply the same eye to.

Outside of work: husband, dad, drummer, follower of Jesus. The real reason any of this exists is pretty simple. We just like people. If we can help a small business finally look the part, that's enough.
