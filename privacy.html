Privacy policy

Short, because we collect almost nothing.

What we collect

If you submit the contact form, we receive what you typed: your name, email, business name, industry, and message. That's it. The form is processed by Formspree, which delivers it to our inbox.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you. Your information is never sold, rented, shared, or added to a mailing list you didn't ask for.

Analytics

We may use privacy standard analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site: pages visited, time on page, and general region. This data is aggregated and never identifies you personally.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies for advertising or tracking across other websites.
