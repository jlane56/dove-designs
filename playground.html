Demo 02 — Fade Up on Scroll

Elements reveal as you scroll.

This fires once when each element enters the viewport. The stagger staggers them in sequence. Change the y value, duration, or ease and see what shifts.
01

gsap.from()

Animates FROM the values you give it back to the element's natural CSS state. The most common pattern for reveal animations.

02

ScrollTrigger

Watches the scroll position and fires animations when elements enter/leave the viewport. The start value controls exactly when.

03

stagger

When multiple elements share one animation call, stagger delays each one slightly. Creates a cascade without writing multiple animations.

Demo 03 — Scrub (scroll-driven)

Scroll drives the animation.

The circle and bar aren't on timers — they're controlled by your scroll position. Scroll slowly and you'll see them move with you. Scroll back up and they reverse.
Demo 04 — Word Reveal Each word slides up from behind its parent container. The overflow:hidden on .word clips the movement so words appear to emerge from a slot rather than floating in from below.
