06   Voice & Tone

Write like this

  • Clear and direct — no jargon
  • Warm but professional
  • Confident without being pushy
  • "I build custom-coded websites"
  • "No templates. No page builders."
  • Short sentences. Lots of breathing room.

Avoid this

  • Corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage")
  • Overly casual or slang
  • Long paragraphs of dense text
  • Vague promises ("best in class")
  • Passive voice ("websites are built by me")
  • Overselling or pressure tactics
